# ELEVARE - Project Overview

## 🎯 Mission Statement

ELEVARE revolutionizes career discovery by replacing traditional one-time assessments with continuous behavioral analysis through AI-powered conversations, providing personalized career recommendations based on longitudinal data patterns.

## 🔬 Research Foundation

### Psychological Models
- **Big Five Personality Model (OCEAN)**: Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, Neuroticism
- **Ikigai Framework**: What you love, What you're good at, What the world needs, What you can be paid for
- **Behavioral Trait Analysis**: 8 core traits tracked over time

### Technical Innovation
- **Longitudinal Analysis**: Tracks behavioral patterns over weeks/months
- **NLP-Powered Insights**: Sentiment analysis, emotion detection, keyword extraction
- **Hybrid Recommendation Engine**: Combines collaborative filtering with content-based matching
- **Explainable AI**: Transparent reasoning for career suggestions
